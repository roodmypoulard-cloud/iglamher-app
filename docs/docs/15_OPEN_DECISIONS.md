# Open Product Decisions

These items require owner confirmation before production launch:

1. Launch geography and default radius
2. Whether customers book instantly or professionals approve requests
3. Platform commission percentage
4. Customer service fee
5. Deposit versus full-payment policy
6. Cancellation windows and refund percentages
7. Professional verification process
8. Mobile-service travel fee model
9. Tax calculation provider
10. Messaging availability before booking
11. Whether professionals can accept cash
12. Dispute evidence and resolution policy
13. Minimum professional age and licensing requirements by location
14. Launch categories beyond hair, makeup, lashes, and styling
15. Customer support hours and escalation process

Do not hard-code unresolved business rules. Put them behind configuration.
