# Brand Guidelines

## Brand name

**iGlamHer**

Use this capitalization in prose. The visual logo may use stylized letterforms.

## Tagline

**BOOK BEAUTY. ANYTIME. ANYWHERE.**

## Brand character

- Luxurious
- Feminine
- Polished
- Welcoming
- Dependable
- Contemporary
- Editorial rather than flashy

## Visual direction

The selected direction is “Design 2 — Soft Luxe.” It uses a nearly black background, warm rose-gold accents, blush calls to action, sophisticated serif headlines, thin line icons, and warm beauty photography.

Avoid:
- Cheap metallic gradients
- Excessive glitter
- Neon pink
- Dense interfaces
- Generic salon clip art
- Overly ornate crowns
- Tiny low-contrast body copy

## Logo usage

Use the supplied selected logo as visual direction. Production requirements:

- Rebuild as clean SVG paths
- Produce horizontal logo, stacked logo, monogram, app icon, light version, dark version, and one-color version
- Maintain clear space equal to the height of the lowercase “i”
- Do not stretch, bevel excessively, add drop shadows, or place over busy photography
- The crown should be refined and secondary, not larger than the wordmark

## Photography

Prioritize high-end portraits and service details featuring diverse women and professionals. Images should use warm skin tones, soft directional lighting, rich blacks, controlled highlights, and minimal background clutter.

## Voice

Direct, warm, confident, and useful.

Preferred:
- “Choose a time”
- “Confirm booking”
- “Your appointment is secured”
- “Discover professionals near you”

Avoid:
- Overly childish language
- Fake urgency
- Empty luxury clichés
