# Product Copy

## Core labels

- Search placeholder: “Search services or professionals”
- Home greeting: “Hello, Beautiful”
- Category heading: “What are you looking for today?”
- Professional section: “Top professionals near you”
- Booking CTA: “Confirm booking”
- Profile CTA: “View availability”
- Empty favorites: “Save professionals you love and find them here.”
- Empty bookings: “Your next beauty moment starts here.”
- Professional CTA: “Grow your beauty business with iGlamHer.”

## Trust copy

- “Secure payments”
- “Verified reviews”
- “Clear pricing before you book”
- “Support when you need it”

Do not claim that every professional is vetted until the operational process actually supports that promise.

## Error tone

Helpful, not cute:
- “That time is no longer available. Choose another time.”
- “Your payment was not completed. You have not been charged.”
- “We could not save your changes. Try again.”

## Notification examples

- Booking confirmed
- Appointment reminder
- Professional requested a new time
- New message
- Booking completed — leave a review
- Payout sent
