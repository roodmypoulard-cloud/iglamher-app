### C3 CAVEAT — NOT fixed by this commit, needs a decision
1. `0003_rls.sql:43` grants public row-read on `professional_profiles` with **no column-level
   restriction**, and `PRO_SELECT` is `select *`. A client hitting PostgREST directly can still
   `select studio_address,location_lat,location_lng` for any active pro. TS-side redaction does not
   protect that endpoint. Proper fix = a redacted public view or column GRANTs; I did **not** attempt
   it because `select *` breaks under column-level privileges and I have no live DB to test against.
2. `formatDistance` (`src/lib/format.ts:16`) emits 0.1 mi precision — enough to trilaterate a hidden
   pin from a few viewer positions. Should be coarsened/bucketed for `hideExactPin` pros.
Both are real leaks. Recommend they land before any claim of "address privacy" ships to customers.
