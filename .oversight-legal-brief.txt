iGlamHer — Professional Registration, Service Location Compliance, and License Verification

Update Professional Mode onboarding and account settings to include service-location eligibility, professional licensing, and document verification.

Do not redesign the entire app. Keep the current iGlamHer luxury rose-gold visual system, typography, navigation, and bottom bar.

The goal is to allow professionals to clearly declare where they work, upload required licenses, and show customers whether their credentials have been verified.

⸻

1. Professional Registration Flow

When a user chooses:

“I am a Beauty Professional”

or switches from Customer Mode into Professional Mode, add a structured professional onboarding process.

Use a multi-step progress flow instead of one long page.

Steps

1. Professional Information
2. Services Offered
3. Service Location
4. License and Credentials
5. Identity Verification
6. Review and Submit

Show a progress indicator at the top:

Step 1 of 6

Users must be able to go back without losing previously entered information.

Save each completed step automatically as a draft.

⸻

STEP 1 — PROFESSIONAL INFORMATION

Collect:

* Legal first name
* Legal last name
* Professional or business name
* Phone number
* Email
* Profile photo
* Professional biography
* Years of experience
* Languages spoken
* Business website or social media, optional
* Business address, if applicable

Add this notice:

Your legal information is used for verification and will not be displayed publicly unless required.

⸻

STEP 2 — SERVICES OFFERED

Allow the professional to select one or multiple categories:

* Hair
* Makeup
* Nails
* Lashes
* Brows
* Skincare
* Barbering
* Bridal Beauty
* Other

After selecting a category, allow the professional to add individual services.

Each service should include:

* Service name
* Description
* Price
* Duration
* Deposit requirement
* Mobile-service fee, optional
* Portfolio examples
* Whether the service requires a professional license

Do not hardcode every service. The system should be scalable so more categories can be added later.

⸻

STEP 3 — WHERE DO YOU PROVIDE SERVICES?

Create a luxury selection page titled:

Where do you provide your services?

Allow multiple selections:

My Home Studio

Description:

Clients travel to your approved home-based beauty studio.

Client’s Location

Description:

You travel to the client’s home, hotel, office, venue, or production location.

Salon or Salon Suite

Description:

You provide services from a licensed salon, salon suite, or shared beauty workspace.

Private Commercial Studio

Description:

You operate from a private commercial beauty studio.

Multiple Locations

Description:

You provide services from more than one approved location.

Use elegant selectable cards with icons, subtle rose-gold borders, and a gold check animation when selected.

⸻

Home Studio Compliance Questions

When My Home Studio is selected, reveal additional required questions:

* Is this location legally permitted to operate as a beauty-service business?
* Do you have permission from the property owner or landlord, if required?
* Does the location comply with local zoning, health, safety, and licensing requirements?
* Is the location inside an HOA or managed property?
* If yes, do the property rules allow client visits?
* Do you hold any required home-salon or establishment license?
* Do you have professional liability insurance?
* Do you have a separate, clean workspace for clients?
* Are pets kept away from the service area during appointments?
* Is there a working restroom available for clients?
* Is the location accessible by a safe and clearly identified entrance?

Use Yes/No selectors.

If the professional answers No to a legally required compliance question, do not automatically approve the location.

Show:

This location may require additional documentation or approval before clients can book services here.

Flag the profile for admin review.

⸻

Professional Certification Checkbox

Before continuing, require the professional to check:

I confirm that I am responsible for obtaining and maintaining all licenses, permits, insurance, zoning approvals, establishment approvals, and other legal requirements needed to provide services at my selected location or locations.

Also require:

I understand that iGlamHer is a marketplace and does not grant permission to operate a beauty business from a home, salon, studio, or client location.

Store the date, time, user ID, IP information if available, and version of the agreement accepted.

⸻

Address Privacy

For home studios:

* Never show the complete address publicly.
* Before booking, show only the approximate neighborhood, city, and distance.
* Reveal the complete address only after the booking and required payment are confirmed.
* Do not expose the unit number in search results.
* Allow the professional to hide the exact map pin before booking.
* Admin users must still be able to view the complete submitted address.

Example customer display:

West Hollywood, CA · Exact address provided after booking

STEP 4 — LICENSE AND CREDENTIALS

Create a dedicated license upload section titled:

Professional License and Credentials

Explain:

Upload the licenses, permits, certifications, and insurance documents required for the services you provide. Requirements vary by service and location.

Allow the professional to add multiple documents.

Each document must include:

* Document type
* License or permit number
* Issuing state
* Issuing authority
* Name shown on document
* Issue date
* Expiration date
* Service category connected to the document
* Front image or PDF
* Back image, if applicable
* Optional supporting document

Document Type Options

* Cosmetology License
* Esthetician License
* Manicurist or Nail Technician License
* Barber License
* Hair Stylist License
* Lash Certification
* Makeup Certification
* Salon Establishment License
* Home Salon Permit
* Business License
* Seller’s Permit
* Professional Liability Insurance
* Mobile Service Permit
* Health or Sanitation Certification
* Other

Allow uploads in:

* PDF
* JPG
* JPEG
* PNG

Set reasonable file-size limits and compress images without making text unreadable.

Users must be able to:

* Preview the document
* Replace it
* Delete it
* Save it as a draft
* Upload additional documents

⸻

License Requirement Logic

License requirements should depend on:

* Professional’s selected services
* Professional’s service location
* State
* City or local jurisdiction
* Whether the professional receives clients at home
* Whether the professional works inside a salon
* Whether the professional travels to customers

Do not pretend the same license rules apply everywhere.

Create a flexible rules structure that can later be updated by the admin.

Example:

If a professional selects Nails in California, the system can request a manicurist or cosmetology license.

If the professional selects Home Studio, the system can also request an establishment license, home occupation permit, or other location documentation where applicable.

If a service does not require a license in a particular jurisdiction, display:

A professional license may not be required for this service in your location. You are still responsible for complying with applicable local laws.

Do not automatically state that a person is legally compliant simply because they uploaded a document.

⸻

STEP 5 — IDENTITY VERIFICATION

Require:

* Government-issued photo ID
* Live selfie or identity verification flow
* Phone verification
* Email verification
* Legal name matching

Compare the professional’s legal name against the name on uploaded professional licenses.

If the names do not match, allow the professional to submit:

* Business documentation
* Marriage or name-change document
* Written explanation

Flag mismatches for admin review.

Do not show government ID publicly.

3. VERIFICATION STATUSES

Each uploaded document must have one of these statuses:

Draft

Uploaded but not submitted.

Submitted

Waiting for review.

Under Review

An admin is reviewing the document.

Verified

Approved and currently valid.

Rejected

Document was invalid, unreadable, expired, mismatched, or insufficient.

Expiring Soon

Expiration is within 30 days.

Expired

The document is no longer valid.

Additional Information Required

Admin needs another document or explanation.

Use distinct status chips but keep them consistent with the luxury iGlamHer visual system.

⸻

4. CUSTOMER-FACING VERIFICATION BADGES

Do not show the professional’s private documents to customers.

Customers should only see approved verification indicators.

Possible public badges:

* Identity Verified
* Phone Verified
* Professional License Verified
* Insured Professional
* Home Studio Reviewed
* Salon Location Verified
* iGlamHer Recommended
* Popular Professional

Important:

The gold checkmark must not represent all types of verification at once.

Use clear labels so customers understand exactly what has been verified.

Example:

Gold checkmark — Professional License Verified

A professional should only receive a verification badge after admin approval.

Do not allow professionals to give themselves badges.

⸻

5. BOOKING RESTRICTIONS

A professional may save their profile before verification, but the platform must restrict bookings when required documentation is missing.

Possible profile states:

Profile Draft

Not publicly visible.

Pending Verification

Profile may be visible, but restricted services cannot be booked.

Approved

Services may be booked.

Partially Approved

Only approved service categories and locations may be booked.

Suspended

Bookings disabled.

Expired Documents

Affected services or locations temporarily disabled.

Example:

A professional may be approved for makeup services but still pending approval for nails.

The system must restrict only the affected service instead of unnecessarily disabling the entire profile.

⸻

6. EXPIRATION MANAGEMENT

Create automatic expiration tracking.

Send notifications:

* 60 days before expiration
* 30 days before expiration
* 7 days before expiration
* On the expiration date

Notification example:

Your Manicurist License expires in 30 days. Upload the renewed license to prevent interruption of nail bookings.

When a required license expires:

* Mark it as Expired
* Remove the related public verification badge
* Temporarily disable affected bookings
* Notify the professional
* Add the issue to the admin dashboard
* Preserve existing confirmed bookings for admin review instead of automatically canceling them

9. SECURITY REQUIREMENTS

* Validate file types on both client and server.
* Scan uploads where supported.
* Reject executable or suspicious files.
* Use private storage buckets.
* Apply role-based access controls.
* Never expose government identification publicly.
* Never expose full home addresses before a confirmed booking.
* Log document access by admins.
* Prevent users from changing verification status through client-side requests.
* Verification decisions must be performed through protected server-side admin actions.
* Rate-limit upload and submission endpoints.
* Preserve an audit history when documents are replaced.

⸻

10. USER EXPERIENCE AND DESIGN

Keep the experience elegant, reassuring, and easy for nontechnical beauty professionals.

Use:

* Soft white or warm cream backgrounds
* Rose-gold accents
* Gold verification checks
* Rounded luxury cards
* Large upload areas
* Clear progress indicators
* Simple explanations
* Smooth page transitions
* Subtle check animations
* Skeleton loaders while documents load
* Clear error messages
* Mobile-first layouts

Do not make the flow feel like a government application.

It should feel premium, trustworthy, simple, and professional.

Use supportive language such as:

Let’s verify your professional credentials.

Your documents are private and securely stored.

Verified credentials help clients feel confident booking you.

Avoid threatening or overly legal language in the main interface.

Place detailed legal terms behind an expandable:

Learn more about professional requirements

⸻

11. IMPORTANT LEGAL PRODUCT LANGUAGE

Do not display language claiming that iGlamHer has legally authorized the professional to operate.

Use:

Verified documents indicate that iGlamHer reviewed the submitted information. Verification does not guarantee that the professional satisfies every law, regulation, permit, zoning requirement, or insurance obligation applicable to their business.

Use:

Professionals are responsible for maintaining all licenses, permits, approvals, and insurance required for their services and operating locations.

Require acceptance of the professional terms before submission.
